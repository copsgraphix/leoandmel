# HOME PAGE — EXPERIENCE GATE (CRITICAL ENTRY POINT)

OBJECTIVE:
Users must choose their experience path before entering the site.

This is a bifurcation screen separating:
- Commercial / Government (Business Experience)
- Personal / Residential (Light Experience or redirect)

DO NOT SKIP THIS SECTION.

---

# STRUCTURE

Full-screen split layout.

Left Side:
BUSINESS / COMMERCIAL / GOVERNMENT

Right Side:
PERSONAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

# LEFT SIDE — BUSINESS EXPERIENCE (PRIMARY PATH)

Visual:
- aerial commercial property
- clean, structured, professional environment
- subtle motion (slow zoom or parallax)

Text:
“Commercial & Government Property Maintenance”

Subtext:
“For organizations managing large-scale properties.”

CTA:
“Enter Business Experience”

---

# RIGHT SIDE — PERSONAL EXPERIENCE

Visual:
- smaller residential lawn
- lighter, simpler maintenance visuals
- calmer tone

Text:
“Personal Property Care”

Subtext:
“For residential and small property needs.”

CTA:
“Enter Personal Experience”

---

# INTERACTION RULES

- Hover expands side (smooth GSAP scale)
- Opposite side slightly fades/dims
- Click locks selection and transitions into that experience

---

# NAVIGATION LOGIC

IF user selects BUSINESS:
→ Load full cinematic scroll experience (Sections 1–7)

IF user selects PERSONAL:
→ Either:
   - simplified version of same system (lightweight flow)
   - OR redirect to separate page (recommended)

---

# TRANSITION ANIMATION

After selection:
- screen splits animate outward
- chosen side expands full screen
- transitions into Section 1 (Hero Transformation)

---

# DESIGN RULES

- No buttons overload
- No traditional navbar at start
- Must feel like “choosing a film experience”
- Keep cinematic tone consistent with rest of site
